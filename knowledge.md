## What are the hours of the library?
The UGA Libraries homepage lists these hours. The Main Library is open 7:30 A.M. to 2:00 A.M. The McBay Science Library is also open 7:30 A.M. to 2:00 A.M. The Miller Learning Center is open 24 hours. The Special Collections Libraries are open 8:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M. Access to the Main Library and the McBay Science Library after 6:00 pm, and to the Miller Learning Center after 9:00 pm, is open to people with a UGA or other College ID only. More hours and locations are on the All Library Hours page and the Locations page.
## How do I borrow a book?
The Borrow & Request menu includes Borrow & Renew, Request a PDF, Request a Book, Suggest a Purchase, GIL Express Borrowing, and Interlibrary Loan. Under My Accounts, My Library Account lets you view and renew books, media, and equipment. The ILL Account (ILLiad) is used for interlibrary loan requests. The Special Collections Research Account (Aeon) is used for Archives and Special Collections requests.
## Where can I find books, and what books does the library have?
Search GIL-Find, the UGA Libraries' catalog for both print and e-books. The Libraries provide access to over 1 million eBooks, and the E-Books Guide explains how to find and open them. To find eBooks, search GIL-Find, filter to "Full Access Online," and select "Books" under Format. eBooks found through Multi-Search are limited to EBSCOhost eBooks. GIL-Find also covers the other University System of Georgia libraries, and GIL Express lets you borrow from them. The UGA Law Library's catalog is called GAVEL. WorldCat lists books owned by libraries worldwide, and Interlibrary Loan can borrow from them. Search the catalog to see which specific books are available. UGA students, faculty, and staff can request a purchase of books they need, and eBook requests are preferred.

## How do I search for articles and journals, and what databases can I use?
The homepage "Start Your Search" area has four options. Multi-Search uses EBSCOhost to find articles, books and more, with Advanced Search and Guest Access. Books & More searches the library catalog for books, journals, media and more. Journals by Title finds a specific journal, magazine or newspaper by title. Google Scholar + UGA Access is described as the power of Google with the full text of UGA. Popular databases listed are ERIC, JSTOR, PsycINFO, PubMed, and Web of Science. You can browse databases by name from A to Z or by subject, and a "What am I searching?" link sits next to the Multi-Search box.
